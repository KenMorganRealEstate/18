The Residence

Experience refined living in this stunning, nearly fully-furnished corner townhome. The main level welcomes you with an open foyer, a convenient two-piece powder room, and seamless access to the garage. The heart of the home, a bright Great Room, overlooks a chef-inspired eat-in kitchen complete with extensive cabinetry and premium stone backsplash. Step through the garden door to a fully fenced, tranquil backyard oasis featuring a deck and stone patio. The upper level offers three spacious bedrooms, including an expansive primary suite with a large walk-in closet and oversized en-suite bathroom. Additional highlights include a versatile open office space, convenient upper-level laundry, and an unspoiled lower level ready for your custom touch.
